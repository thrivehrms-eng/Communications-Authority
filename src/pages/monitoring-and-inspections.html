The Authority makes sure licensees keep to their licence terms and conditions, in line with sections 26 and 86 of the Kenya Information and Communications Act, 1998. To do this it regularly monitors and inspects licensees’ activities.

What monitoring and inspection covers

  • Quality of Service (QoS) monitoring and analysis, and publishing the reports for the public.

  • Inspecting licensees’ operations to establish compliance with licence terms and conditions.

  • Monitoring and inspecting spectrum resources to resolve interference and establish how licensees use their spectrum.

  • Monitoring broadcast content for compliance with the Programming Code, licence conditions and regulatory requirements.

  • Surveillance and inspections to make sure telecommunications equipment installations meet the set standards.

  • Surveillance and inspections of broadcast licensees to make sure they keep to content standards.

  • Inspecting licensee infrastructure to confirm its roll-out meets licence roll-out targets.

  • Liaising with county governments, ICT stakeholders and government ministries, departments and agencies (MDAs) on the provision of ICT services.

Monitoring and inspection reports are a critical input to the Authority’s regulatory actions and help keep the ICT sector working in harmony.
