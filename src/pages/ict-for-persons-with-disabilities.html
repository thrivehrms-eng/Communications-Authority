Recognising the ICT needs of persons with disabilities (PwDs), the Authority started a project to provide them with ICT solutions.

The programme includes:

  • Setting up school-based ICT centres in post-primary institutions for PwDs;
  • Creation of an accessibility web portal to provide information for and on PwDs in the country; and
  • Conducting an awareness campaign on ICT and PwDs with a view of developing guidelines on ICT and PwDs.

The Authority identified eight institutions for people with physical, hearing and visual impairments to join the programme.
