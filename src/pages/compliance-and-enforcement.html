The Authority enforces compliance with licence conditions under sections 41, 43, 46A and 83A of the Kenya Information and Communications Act, 1998.

Compliance returns are due every year

All active ICT sector licensees are expected to file compliance returns annually. The Authority renews operating licences based on licensees’ compliance with their licence conditions, as assessed from these returns.

What enforcement involves

  • Monitoring the day-to-day performance of licensees to ensure compliance with licence terms and conditions, guidelines and the Act.
  • Gathering, analysing and publishing the results of compliance returns from licensees.
  • Monitoring communication infrastructure roll-out against licence roll-out targets, and analysing spectrum utilisation returns.
  • Addressing escalated competition and consumer complaints through appropriate enforcement mechanisms.
  • Reviewing surveillance findings to take enforcement action, and making sure all licensees keep to technical and operational standards.
  • Preparing compliance reports that inform the issue of compliance certificates or the imposition of penalties.

ICT indicators

The Authority regularly prepares sector statistics reports on the performance of the ICT industry in Kenya, in line with International Telecommunication Union standards for collecting administrative (supply-side) data. These reports measure the availability and accessibility of ICT services from the supply side; demand-side data on the use and uptake of ICTs is collected separately through National ICT Surveys, with the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics.
