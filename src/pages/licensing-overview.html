At a glance

Licensing every communications system and service.

The Communications Authority of Kenya (CA) is mandated to license all communications systems and services in the country.

In executing this and its other responsibilities, the Authority is guided by relevant statutes, including the Kenya Information and Communications Act, 1998.

Applying for a licence

How licensing works.

For telecommunications, postal and courier licences, the process starts with a duly completed application form, in English, addressed to the Authority. Once the application is accepted:

  1. Pay the application fee

    The prescribed application fee is non-refundable.

  2. Committee review

    The Communication Licensing Committee considers the application.

  3. Public notice

    If endorsed, it is published in the Kenya Gazette for at least 30 days so interested parties can comment.

  4. Board decision

    The Board then considers the application. If approved, you are advised of the offer conditions and licence fees.

  5. Licence issued

    The licence is issued once the conditions in the offer letter are met.

Universal access

Reaching areas the market does not.

The Universal Service Fund, administered by the Authority, finances projects that bring ICT services to rural, remote and poor urban areas.

    78 sub-locations

  • Education broadband

    All 47 counties

  • E-resource centres

    56 public libraries

    Equipped with computers, connectivity and accessibility software.
