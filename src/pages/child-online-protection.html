The Authority takes cognisance of the fact that children are vulnerable in cyberspace.

In 2011, it held a multi-stakeholder workshop, “Protecting Children in Cyberspace: Whose Responsibility Is It?”, to engender conversation among stakeholders on securing children online.

In 2015, the Authority rolled out a Child Online Protection (COP) programme, bringing together stakeholders to equip children and their parents or guardians with the information and skills to practise safe internet use and minimise exposure to risks and vulnerabilities.

Over the years, this campaign has triggered the development of other initiatives, services and mechanisms that ensure child safety online.
