To increase access to ICT services, the Authority and the Kenya National Library Service (KNLS) established e-resource centres in ten community libraries in 2012.

The Authority provided furniture, hardware and software, and internet connectivity for two years, all valued at KSh 24 million.

Second phase

In the second phase, the Authority is supporting the establishment of 46 more e-resource centres across the country, contributing KSh 167 million for furniture, computer hardware and software, local area networks and two years of internet connectivity.
