Public awareness campaigns the Authority runs from time to time to inform consumers of ICT services.

  • Chukua Hatua, for mobile and internet users.

  • Kaa Macho, for postal and courier consumers.

  • Awareness campaigns on SIM card registration, mobile number portability, counterfeit equipment, and the migration from analogue to digital broadcasting.

Find out more, and download the Authority’s consumer awareness materials, under Consumer Education and Outreach.
