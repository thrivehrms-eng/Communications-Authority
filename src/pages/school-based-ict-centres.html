The Authority supported the establishment of 16 school-based ICT centres, two in each of the eight provinces. Their main objective is to build ICT capacity and skills in the country.

The strategy is informed by the high absorption and diffusion rate of new skills and technology within the students’ age-group. Moreover, there is already in place an IT curriculum from the Ministry of Education that is to facilitate immediate uptake of the training. The centres are expected to serve the school and neighbouring communities.

In this project, the Authority supplied each selected school with a server, four computers and a printer. In addition to this, the Authority supported the cost of servicing and maintenance for one year effective from May 2007, including the provision of free Internet connectivity. As part of their contribution to this project, telecommunication operators in the country committed to provide free Internet connectivity for at least 12 months.
