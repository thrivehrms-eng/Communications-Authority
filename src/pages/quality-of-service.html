Communications networks do not always have the capacity to handle every simultaneous request, so consumers cannot always expect the highest possible level of service and must accept a degree of variability. Even so, consumers are entitled to basic levels of service.

Basic levels of service

  1. Value for money.

  2. Accurate billing.

  3. Ease of use of the service or product.

  4. Professionalism, on the part of the service provider, in delivering the service.

  5. Flexibility in use of the service, for instance the ease of switching from one operator, resource or piece of equipment to another, or changing a telephone number.

  6. Performance according to specified expectations.

  7. Reliability, and fulfilment of the consumer’s specified needs.

  8. Security and privacy of all data sent and received.
