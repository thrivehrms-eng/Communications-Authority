The Kenya Information and Communications (Amendment) Act, 2009, provides for a Universal Service Fund (USF), administered and managed by the Communications Authority of Kenya.

  • Purpose

    To support widespread access to ICT services and promote capacity building and innovation in ICT services in the country.

  • Sources

    Levies on licensees, appropriations from Government, and grants and donations.

  • What it finances

    National projects with significant impact on the availability and accessibility of ICTs in rural, remote and poor urban areas.
