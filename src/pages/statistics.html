The Authority evaluates and monitors the performance and trends of the communications sector through market analysis and quarterly reporting of data collected from service providers, as required by their licence conditions.

These quarterly sector statistics reports are a reliable source of information for researchers, academia, policymakers, prospective investors, other regulators and the general public.

Queries and disclaimer

Queries on the reports can be directed to info@ca.go.ke. Although every effort is made to ensure accuracy, the Authority is not liable for inaccuracies in this data, which is contingent upon operators’ and service providers’ compliance returns.
