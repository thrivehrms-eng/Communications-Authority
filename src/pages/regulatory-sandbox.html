The regulatory sandbox is an alternative regulatory tool that lets innovators test emerging ICT products and services in a controlled environment.

It enables closer collaboration between ICT innovators and the Authority, which is responsible for developing the information and communications sectors, including broadcasting, cybersecurity, multimedia, telecommunications, electronic commerce, and postal and courier services, and for safeguarding the interests of consumers of ICT products and services.

Examples of products and services that can be tested

  • Innovative telecommunication solutions: new methods of data transmission, or innovative mobile technologies and applications.

  • Cybersecurity tools: software or hardware that protects users and businesses from cyber threats.

  • IoT devices: new internet-connected devices, such as smart home systems or industrial IoT solutions.

  • E-health solutions: telemedicine platforms, wearable health monitoring devices using licensed spectrum, or health record management systems.

  • E-learning platforms: platforms that use advanced technologies such as AI to personalise learning.

  • Drone technologies: delivery, surveillance, agriculture or media broadcasting.

  • AI-driven services: platforms or tools that apply artificial intelligence to the ICT and telecommunications sector.

  • New broadcasting technologies: new ways of delivering content to audiences.

  • Smart city solutions: urban planning, traffic management, waste management and other city functions that use ICT.

  • Digital identity solutions: secure digital identity verification for ICT services.

Apply through the Regulatory Sandbox portal (external site). See also Innovations for the Authority’s wider innovation programmes, including the Global Innovation and Entrepreneurship Centre (GIEC).
