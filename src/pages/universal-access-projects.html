Since 2017, the Authority has initiated a number of pilot projects to inform the nationwide implementation of universal access projects in future.

  • Voice Infrastructure Project

    78 sub-locations covered by mobile communication services in the first phase.

  • Education Broadband Connectivity Project

    Secondary schools in all 47 counties connected to high-speed (5 Mbps) internet.

  • E-Resource Centres

    With the Kenya National Library Service, equipping public libraries with computers, connectivity and accessibility software.

  • E-Justice

    Local area networks in 42 court stations across 28 counties.

  • Research and Development

    Continuous studies and research to inform timely interventions towards universal access.

  • Computerisation of Health Centres

    With the Provincial Medical Office of Nairobi, the Ministry of Health, Telkom Kenya and other partners.

  • Digitisation of the KCSE Curriculum

    Relevant local content, as a demand driver for ICT services.

  • ICT for Persons with Disabilities

    ICT solutions for people with physical, hearing and visual impairments.

  • Community Telecentres

    Community access points in rural and high-cost areas, reducing the cost of owning equipment and services.

  • School-Based ICT Centres

    16 school-based ICT centres, two in each of the eight former provinces.
