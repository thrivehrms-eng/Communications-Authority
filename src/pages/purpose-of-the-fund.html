The Kenya Information and Communications (Amendment) Act, 2009, provides for a Universal Service Fund (USF), administered and managed by the Communications Authority of Kenya.

The purpose of the Fund is to support widespread access to ICT services and promote capacity building and innovation in ICT services in the country. Its sources include levies on licensees, appropriations from Government, and grants and donations. The Fund is expected to finance national projects with significant impact on the availability and accessibility of ICTs in rural, remote and poor urban areas.
