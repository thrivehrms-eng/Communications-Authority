A code of practice is a set of professional standards and written guidelines, established by members of a profession or by official bodies, to help ensure ethical conduct.

Codes are useful where government regulation is unsuitable or unlikely for a particular part of the market, where they would raise industry standards alongside overarching legislation, or where there is a wide commitment to controls that give customers more than the minimum requirements.

They work as industry self-regulation, strengthening consumer confidence, advancing good business practice and encouraging ethical conduct in the communications sector. The Authority works with stakeholders to develop codes that promote ethical conduct among service providers, creating a good environment for communications companies while ensuring quality, affordable services for Kenyan consumers.
