The Geographic Information Systems (GIS) portal is a one-stop, interactive online platform created by the Authority to show the different layers of ICT coverage in Kenya.

The Geo-Portal is updated regularly. It helps the Authority identify service-area gaps by giving GPS coordinates for selected Universal Service clusters and areas that need intervention, and is used to determine the extent of communication coverage and citizens’ access.

What the map shows

  • Telecom (3G and 2G) coverage from the three operators
  • The postal network
  • Broadcasting
  • Fibre routes
  • Transport
  • Administrative boundaries
  • Education and health facilities
  • Electricity networks

Open the ICT Services Coverage Geo-Portal (external site). Licensees and Authority staff use the same portal with their assigned credentials.
