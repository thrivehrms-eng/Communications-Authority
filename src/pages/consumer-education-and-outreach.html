Technology changes fast, and consumers are not always able to keep up with its implications. Educating consumers is an effective way to protect them, helping them make informed choices when purchasing and using ICTs.

Past outreach programmes include ‘Chukua Hatua’ (targeting mobile and internet users) and ‘Kaa Macho’ (targeting postal/courier consumers), alongside awareness campaigns on SIM card registration, mobile number portability, counterfeit equipment, and the migration from analogue to digital broadcasting.

Consumer forums: Kikao Kikuu

Public events (“barazas”) held in accessible locations since 2014, giving consumers the opportunity to interact directly with communication service providers on issues of concern.

Child Online Protection

Children are early adopters of ICTs yet remain among the most vulnerable consumers. In 2011 the Authority held a multi-stakeholder workshop on protecting children in cyberspace, and in 2015 rolled out its Child Online Protection (COP) programme to equip children and their parents or guardians with the skills to practise safe internet use. See the Child Online Protection page.

Workshops

The Authority holds workshops with stakeholders on subjects of consumer interest, including “Women, Girls and ICT” (2013), which explored the access and use challenges women and girls face, and “E-Accessibility for Persons with Disabilities” (2012), which led to the launch of a web portal for persons with disabilities in partnership with the National Council for Persons with Disabilities and the United Disabled Persons of Kenya.
