Public notice

Promotion and use of the dot KE domain name by the Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) sector

The Communications Authority of Kenya (CA) is the regulatory agency for the ICT industry in Kenya, with responsibilities in telecommunications, cybersecurity, e-commerce, broadcasting and postal/courier services. CA also manages the country’s numbering and frequency spectrum resources, administers the Universal Service Fund (USF), and safeguards the interests of users of ICT services.

Pursuant to section 83 of the Kenya Information and Communications Act, 1998, CA has licensed the Kenya Network Information Centre (KeNIC) as the entity responsible for managing and administering the dot KE country-code top-level domain (ccTLD), serving as the Registry and overseeing registration, allocation and maintenance of Kenya’s Domain Name System.

The National Computer and Cybercrimes Coordination Committee (NC4), a multi-agency entity established by the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018, made a determination on 14th March 2024 directing that all systems designated as critical information infrastructure (per Gazette Notice No. 1043) must adopt and only use dot KE domain names, and ensure the security and reliability of any dot KE domains in use. The list of licensed dot KE registrars is available from KeNIC’s website (external site). This directive was to be implemented within six months of the notice.
