Communications equipment sold, distributed or used in Kenya, such as phones, routers and other radio or terminal equipment, must be type approved by the Authority. Type approval confirms it meets national technical, safety and network-compatibility standards before it reaches the market.

Type approval is done once for each equipment model. It covers items such as mobile cellular devices (2G–5G and future generations), PSTN equipment (PABX, fax and telephone handsets), radio communication equipment, data equipment (routers, modems and switches) and digital set-top boxes.

Only licensed vendors and contractors may sell equipment

They must obtain type approval for each model before marketing or selling it. Check the list of type-approved equipment before you apply.

How to apply

Submit the application form for type approval or acceptance together with:

  • a letter of agency from the manufacturer or principal distributor
  • a sample of the equipment
  • technical and user manuals in English
  • conformance certificates and test reports from an accredited laboratory
  • a valid copy of your licence from the Authority, or evidence of compliance
  • proof of payment of the non-refundable fee

The Authority evaluates the equipment using the laboratory and manufacturer’s test reports and technical documentation, and does not have to return the documentation. If the equipment complies with the mandatory standards, a Provisional Type Approval or Authority to Use is granted. If the application is rejected, the applicant is informed.
