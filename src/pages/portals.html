The Authority runs a number of online portals for licensing, spectrum and other regulatory services, giving licensees and the public direct digital access.

  • Licensee Register

    Search the Authority’s current licensees.

  • Staff Verification Portal

    Confirm whether someone claiming to represent the Authority is a genuine member of staff.

  • Regulatory Sandbox (external site)

    Apply to test emerging ICT products and services in a controlled environment.

  • Global Innovation and Entrepreneurship Centre (GIEC) (external site)

    Kenya’s ITU Acceleration Centre.

  • ICT Services Coverage Geo-Portal (external site)

    An interactive map of ICT coverage across Kenya.

  • Numbering Management System (external site)

    Used for numbering resource applications and assignments.

You can also file a complaint or register as a supplier directly through this website.
