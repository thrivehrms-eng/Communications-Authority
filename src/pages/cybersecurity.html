The Kenya Information and Communications Act, 1998, mandates the Authority to develop a national cybersecurity management framework.

To mitigate cyber threats and foster a safer Kenyan cyberspace, the government established the National Kenya Computer Incident Response Team – Coordination Centre (National KE-CIRT/CC), a multi-agency collaboration framework that acts as Kenya’s national point of contact on cybersecurity matters. The Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018, strengthened this framework, among other measures that support national cybersecurity resilience.

The National KE-CIRT/CC

The National KE-CIRT/CC coordinates the national response to cybersecurity matters with relevant actors locally and internationally. Based at the CA Centre, it brings together staff from the Authority and law enforcement agencies, and has detected, prevented and responded to cyber threats around the clock since 2017. It is the interface between local and international ICT service providers whose platforms are used to commit cybercrimes and the Judicial, Law and Order Sector, which investigates and prosecutes them.

Its functions include

  • Implementing national cybersecurity policies, laws and regulations, cybersecurity awareness and capacity building, and early warnings and technical advisories on cyber threats, 24 hours a day.
  • Technical coordination of and response to cyber incidents, 24 hours a day, with actors locally and internationally.
  • Developing and implementing a National Public Key Infrastructure (NPKI).
  • Research and development in cybersecurity, and promoting the efficient management of critical internet resources.
