In carrying out its mandate, the Communications Authority of Kenya (CA) gathers a wide range of information, including about its staff, processes, policies, licensee data and financial status. This is known as official information.

The Access to Information Act, 2016, requires Government agencies, including the Authority, to make official information more freely available, to provide for proper access by each person to official information relating to that person, and to protect official information to the extent consistent with the public interest.

In conformance with this requirement, the Authority has established guidelines on access to information, providing a framework for how such requests should be handled.
