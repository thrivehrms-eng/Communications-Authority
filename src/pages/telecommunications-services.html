Application forms and fee schedules for every telecommunications, broadcasting, postal and courier, type approval, numbering, frequency spectrum and electronic certification licence the Authority issues.

Telecom licensing

One universal application form for postal, courier and telecommunications licences now covers every telecommunications, postal and courier licence category, replacing the earlier category-specific forms.

Broadcasting services

Fees for broadcasting services are charged according to the licence category and where the service is delivered.

Postal and courier services

Postal and courier licence fees depend on the service offered, its format and the area served. The licence categories, terms and fees are set out on the Market Structure page; they are reviewed from time to time.

Type approval of communications equipment

On 6 October 2011 the Authority reviewed its regulatory fees, including type approval fees, with an average reduction of 80% intended to encourage vendors and contractors to seek type approval. See Type Approval for what needs approval.

Numbering resources

All licensed operators that need numbers for their operations are eligible for assignment from the national numbering resource. Organisations intending to become licensed, whose licence has been agreed in principle, may reserve numbers for planning for up to three months. End users and service providers that need numbers seek secondary assignment from eligible holders, on a transparent, non-discriminatory and equitable basis.

All licensed mobile operators may administer short numbers with leading digits 2–8 for their own customers’ text messaging, subject to audit. Short codes beginning with 1 and 9 are reserved for customer services and emergency services and are assigned only by the Authority.

Frequency spectrum resources

Frequency fees depend on the type of service offered. To apply for radio communication services, complete the application form for the service you want to provide.

Electronic certification service providers

An Electronic Certification Service Provider (E-CSP) is licensed to issue, verify, store, renew, suspend and revoke digital certificates to subscribers securely, and may begin issuing digital certificates only once accredited by the Authority.
