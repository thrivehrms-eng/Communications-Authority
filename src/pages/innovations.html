The Authority supports innovation by creating an enabling environment for developing, testing and adopting emerging technologies and digital solutions.

Through initiatives such as the Regulatory Sandbox and the Global Innovation and Entrepreneurship Centre (GIEC), it fosters collaboration, experimentation and entrepreneurship to drive responsible innovation and Kenya’s digital transformation.

Regulatory Sandbox

The regulatory sandbox is an alternative regulatory tool that lets innovators test emerging ICT products and services in a controlled environment, enabling closer collaboration between ICT innovators and the Authority. It is open to innovations such as telecommunication solutions, cybersecurity tools, IoT devices, e-health and e-learning platforms, drone technologies, AI-driven services, new broadcasting technologies, smart city solutions and digital identity solutions.

Global Innovation and Entrepreneurship Centre (GIEC)

GIEC is Kenya’s ITU Acceleration Centre, hosted by the Communications Authority of Kenya. It works to bridge the digital innovation divide by building innovation capacity at global and local levels, supporting Kenya’s transition to an innovation-driven digital economy through policy experimentation, entrepreneurship programmes and technology innovation initiatives aligned with Kenya’s Vision 2030 and national digital transformation goals. GIEC is part of a wider network of national, regional and global ITU Acceleration Centres.
