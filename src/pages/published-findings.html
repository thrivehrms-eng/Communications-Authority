The Constitution of Kenya, 2010, requires public participation in policy making, so that policies reflect the greater public good as far as possible.

In executing its mandate as a public institution, the Authority regularly undertakes public consultations on diverse regulatory initiatives. These are the published findings from those consultations.

  • Analysis of Stakeholders’ Comments on the Consultation Paper on the Proposed Introduction of a Permit Processing Fee on the National Electronic Single Window System (NESWS), March 2026

  • Revised Framework for Measurement of Quality of Service (QoS) for Mobile Telecommunication Services, including the analysis matrix on comments received

  • Postal and Courier Sector Published Findings, May 2026 – Revised Postal and Courier Market Structure, analysis matrix on comments received, and Regulatory Impact Statement

  • Summary of received submissions for the Stakeholder Validation Forum on the Proposed National Telecommunication Numbering, Naming, Addressing and Identification (NNAI) Resources Framework

  • Published findings on the Review of the Telecommunications Market Structure – analysis matrix, stakeholder validation report, revised market structure (February 2026) and transitional provisions

  • Analysis of Comments from Public Consultation on Technical Minimum Requirements for DVB-T2 Receivers for the Kenya Market, 2025

  • Analysis of Comments received on Guidelines on the Application and Usage of DVB Identifiers in DTT Networks in Kenya, 2025

  • Comments on the Environmental and Social Impact Assessment Guidelines for ICT Projects

  • Comments on the Framework for Reduction of Carbon Dioxide in the ICT Sector

  • Comments on the RF-EMF Exposure Assessment Framework
