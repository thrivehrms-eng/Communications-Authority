Broadcasting application fees

  1. Get a Payment Instruction Form

    For community and public broadcasting applications, submit your completed form at the front desk, Ground Floor, Wing B, CA Centre, Waiyaki Way, to obtain a Payment Instruction Form.

  2. Pay through eCitizen

    Open the Authority’s eCitizen portal and follow its payment instructions for your application.

  3. Ask Accounts if you need help

    Contact accounts@ca.go.ke. The published Accounts office hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 am–12:00 noon and 2:00 pm–4:00 pm, excluding public holidays.

These instructions are published in CA’s broadcasting application forms. For another service, ask Accounts to confirm the payment process and reference before paying.
