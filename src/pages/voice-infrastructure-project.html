The voice infrastructure project targets areas that are not served, or poorly served, with a mobile signal covering less than 50% of the population, primarily in the Rift Valley and North Eastern regions of the country as well as considerable areas in the Southwest border areas of Kajiado and Narok.

The Universal Service Fund (USF) is committed to ensuring that all the country’s citizens have access to modern, high quality and affordable communication services. To help meet the universal access objective, the voice infrastructure project focuses primarily on voice services in the identified sub-locations.

These sub-locations were considered of highest priority amongst underserved sub-locations, based on their unserved population ratios and a financial analysis projecting their sustainability after the application of one-time smart subsidies.

The project has brought significant benefits, such as easier communication and access to information, convenience, free digital products and new forms of leisure, and social benefits that have improved access to finance, education, health and public services.
