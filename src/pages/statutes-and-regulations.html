The Communications Authority of Kenya operates under the Kenya Information and Communications Act, 1998, which sets the regulatory framework for Kenya’s communications sector.

Parliament enacted the Act in 1998 to implement the Postal and Telecommunications Sector Policy Statement released by the Ministry of Transport and Communications in January 1997. The government updated the Policy Statement in 1999 and again in March 2006. The Act is carried out through a comprehensive set of regulations, and all sector legislation, policies and regulations are aligned to the Constitution of Kenya, 2010.

After the Constitution was promulgated in 2010, existing laws were reviewed for compliance with it. The Act was revised to align with Articles 33 and 34 of the Constitution, which protect freedom of expression and freedom of the media. The Constitution required an independent regulator, free from political, commercial and government influence, which led to the Kenya Information and Communications (Amendment) Act, 2013.
