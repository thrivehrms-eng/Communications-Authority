Who we are

Kenya's ICT regulator, in the public interest.

The Communications Authority of Kenya (CA) is the regulatory agency for the ICT sector in Kenya, established by the Kenya Information and Communications Act, CAP 411A (KICA). We oversee telecommunications, radio communications, broadcasting, electronic transactions, the country's numbering and frequency spectrum resources, and postal and courier services.

CA is also mandated to protect the consumers of these services. Learn about our consumer education and outreach and our work on universal access.
