Procurement

Tenders, awards and how to become a supplier to the Authority.

View tendersRegister as a supplier

At a glance

Doing business with the Authority.

The Communications Authority of Kenya procures goods, works and services in line with the Public Procurement and Asset Disposal Act, 2015.

Prospective suppliers can review current opportunities, register their interest, and follow the outcomes of past procurement.

Current opportunities

There are no open tenders at the moment

Past tenders and their documents are listed on the Tenders page. To be considered for future opportunities, register as a supplier.

See all tenders

For suppliers

How procurement works here.

  1. Register as a supplier

    Register your business with the Authority to be considered for pre-qualification and future tender opportunities.

  2. Check the procurement plan

    The consolidated procurement plan sets out what the Authority plans to buy in the financial year.

  3. Respond to tenders

    Each tender lists its advert, tender document and any addenda, with its opening and closing dates.

  4. Follow the outcomes

    Awarded contracts and certificates of award are published once procurement processes are concluded.
