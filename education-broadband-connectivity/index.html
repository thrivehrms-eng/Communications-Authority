Education Broadband Connectivity

High-speed internet for public secondary schools in all 47 counties.

The Education Broadband Connectivity Project has connected 887 secondary schools across the 47 counties to high-speed (5 Mbps) internet. It is part of the Universal Service Fund’s work to improve education through digital learning.

In supporting broadband expansion, the Fund has provided internet connectivity to public secondary schools in every county. As regional representation could not be equal across all 47 counties, affirmative action was applied in some, especially Mandera, Turkana, Wajir, Garissa, Kwale, Isiolo, Samburu and Tana River, so that every county has a beneficiary school.

The project has integrated ICTs into learning and improved the quality of teaching, especially in public secondary schools. It was implemented in partnership with the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MoEST) under a formal cooperation agreement.
